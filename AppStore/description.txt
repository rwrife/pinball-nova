Pinball Nova is a local-first 3D pinball game with original tables, physics-honest flippers, and modern arcade effects. Play on Windows with keyboard, mouse, or gamepad, and on phones and tablets with touch controls.

Planned features:
- Two original tables: a quick classic layout and a lane-chain table with a multiball wizard mode.
- Bumpers, slingshots, targets, rollovers, ball save, and multiball.
- A personal record book of high scores and longest multiballs, stored only on your device.
- Settings for flipper strength, screen shake, flash intensity, remappable controls, and colorblind-safe palettes.
- Optional tilt-nudge on mobile, always with a button or keyboard alternative.

No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, and no network connection required. Your records and settings never leave your device.

Status: in development. This description lists planned features and will be updated as each one ships.
